# Templates

The default templates are English-first and live under `templates/`.

## Full Recovery

- `templates/full/SPEC.md`
- `templates/full/SPEC-annotated.md`
- `templates/full/DESIGN.md`

These templates guide full baseline recovery. The generated artifacts should become:

- `metaspec/specs/spec.md`
- `metaspec/specs/design.md`

## Incremental Change

- `templates/delta/proposal.md`
- `templates/delta/delta-spec.md`
- `templates/delta/delta-design.md`
- `templates/delta/tasks.md`
- `templates/delta/validation.md`

These templates support the five-stage change flow.

## Extensions

- `templates/extension/service-context.md`
- `templates/extension/guidelines/coding.md`
- `templates/extension/guidelines/testing.md`

Extension templates are optional project policy documents. They are not copied automatically into a target repository.

Chinese reference material is available under `docs/zh-CN/`.
